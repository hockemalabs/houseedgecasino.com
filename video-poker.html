Strategy primer

Jacks or Better strategy, simplified

Hold the highest-ranked pattern you have from the list below. When a hand matches more than one line, keep the one that appears first.

  1. Royal flush, straight flush, four of a kind: hold everything.
  2. Four to a royal flush.
  3. Full house, flush, straight, three of a kind.
  4. Four to a straight flush.
  5. Two pair.
  6. High pair (jacks or better).
  7. Three to a royal flush.
  8. Four to a flush.
  9. Low pair.
  10. Four to an outside straight.
  11. Two suited high cards.
  12. One or two high cards, then discard everything.

Always play max coins. The royal flush pays 800 to 1 at five coins but only 250 to 1 at fewer, and that difference alone costs more than a full percent of return.

What does 9/6 mean in video poker?

It refers to the payout for a full house (9 coins per coin bet) and a flush (6 coins). The 9/6 Jacks or Better table is the full-pay version, returning 99.54% with optimal play.

Why does the app recommend holding a low pair over a flush draw?

Because the math says so. A low pair returns more on average than four to a flush. Learn Mode shows the expected return for each option so you can see the difference.

Does it play with real randomness?

Yes. Each hand is dealt from a freshly shuffled 52-card deck using true random outcomes. There is no rigging in either direction.
