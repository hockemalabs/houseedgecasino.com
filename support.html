How do I restore my Full Version purchase on a new device?

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple Account used for the original purchase. Family Sharing is supported, so family members can also restore it.

My balance is stuck at $100 in the free version. Is that a bug?

No. The free version caps the bankroll at $100 and resets it to $25 when you bust. The Full Version Unlock removes the cap and lets you reset to $100 anytime.

How do I turn strategy hints on or off?

Open Settings from any game and choose a Learn Mode level: Off, Hints, or Tutorial. Blackjack and video poker support real-time optimal play hints.

Does the app need an internet connection?

No. Everything runs on your device. Game Center features such as leaderboards use Apple's services when available.
