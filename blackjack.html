Strategy primer

Basic strategy in sixty seconds

Basic strategy is the set of plays that minimizes the house edge for every combination of your hand and the dealer's up card. It was derived from the math of the game, not from hunches, and it is the same chart professionals memorize.

  • Always split aces and eights. Never split tens or fives.
  • Stand on hard 17 or higher. Stand on hard 12 through 16 when the dealer shows 2 through 6; otherwise hit.
  • Double hard 11 against anything, and hard 10 against a dealer 2 through 9.
  • Hit soft 17 or lower. Double soft 13 through 18 against weak dealer cards when allowed.
  • Decline insurance. It is a side bet with a house edge above 7% in a six-deck game.
