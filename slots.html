Strategy primer

What every slot player should know

Slot machines are the most popular game in the casino and the least understood. A few facts from the app's "Understanding Casino Games" lessons:

  • The house edge on slots is typically 2% to 15%, far higher than blackjack or video poker. The app shows the edge for its Vegas mode up front.
  • Every spin is independent. A machine is never "due." Streaks, hot and cold, are just what randomness looks like.
  • Speed of play matters. Slots run far more rounds per hour than table games, so the edge compounds faster. The app's Time Limits lesson explains the effect.
  • Volatility is a choice. High-volatility games pay rarely but big. Low-volatility games pay often but small. Neither changes the long-run return.

Fun mode exists because sometimes you just want to see the reels light up. The app labels it clearly so you always know which odds you are playing.
