What roulette can and cannot teach you

Roulette is a pure game of chance. No betting system changes the house edge, and the app says so plainly. What you can learn is how each bet behaves:

Every bet has the same edge, so the only real choices are volatility and pace. Even-money bets keep a bankroll alive longest; straight-up bets swing hardest. The app's Learn Mode makes those trade-offs concrete.

FAQ

Roulette questions

Is the roulette wheel in the app random?

Yes. The wheel is driven by a physics simulation with a truly random outcome. It is not weighted toward or against the player.

Why American roulette instead of European?

American double-zero wheels are what most US casinos offer, so the app teaches the version you are most likely to encounter. The tutorials explain how the second zero raises the house edge from 2.70% to 5.26%.

Can I practice a betting system?

You can place any combination of bets and watch how a system behaves over hundreds of spins without risking anything. That is usually the fastest way to see why systems fail.
